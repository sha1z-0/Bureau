Copperline Payments Careers

Software Engineer I

Engineering · Remote (Americas, Europe, South Asia) · Full-Time

Copperline Payments helps independent pharmacies accept card and insurance co-pay payments at the counter and online. We process USD 900M a year for 3,000 pharmacies.

What you'll work on

  • Payment and refund flows in our TypeScript/Node.js services
  • Reconciliation jobs that match card network settlements to pharmacy ledgers
  • Internal tools for our support team

What you bring

  • 1-3 years of professional software engineering experience
  • Strong fundamentals in one backend language and SQL
  • Care for correctness: money has to add up

Compensation: USD 28,000-38,000 for contractors outside the US, depending on location and experience.
