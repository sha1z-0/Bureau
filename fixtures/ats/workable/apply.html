Description

Brightwater Health runs appointment booking, e-prescriptions and lab-result delivery for 220 clinics across Punjab. Our patient app handles 60,000 bookings a week, and our clinic dashboard is open on reception desks from 8am to midnight.

Requirements

  • Working knowledge of PostgreSQL and REST API design
  • Comfortable writing tests and reading logs when something breaks
  • Good written English; Urdu is a plus for clinic-facing work

Benefits

  • Health insurance for you and your family
  • Paid certification courses
