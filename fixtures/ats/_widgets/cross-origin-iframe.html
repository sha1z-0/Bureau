Dispatch Systems Engineer

Rotterdam or remote (CET +/- 5h) · Engineering · Full-time

About the role

Halcyon Freight moves refrigerated containers between 40 ports. Our dispatch platform assigns trucks, slots and cold-chain checks in real time, and it has to keep working when a terminal's API goes quiet for an hour.

  • Build and operate Node.js/TypeScript services behind the dispatch board
  • Model schedules and capacity in PostgreSQL
  • Join the on-call rotation once you're settled (paid, one week in six)
