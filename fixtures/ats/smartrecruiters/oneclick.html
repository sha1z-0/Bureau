Graduate Software Engineer

Meridian Freight · Hamburg, Germany · Remote (EMEA/South Asia) · Full-time

Company Description

Meridian Freight is a digital freight forwarder moving ocean and air cargo between Europe and South Asia. Our platform books, tracks and documents 40,000 shipments a year for 1,200 shippers.

Job Description

As a Graduate Software Engineer in our Shipment Visibility team you will build the services that turn carrier events into the live timeline our customers rely on.

  • Build and maintain TypeScript/Node.js services and PostgreSQL schemas behind shipment tracking
  • Write integration tests against carrier APIs and our event pipeline
  • Ship small features end to end with a senior engineer as your buddy

Qualifications

  • Degree in Computer Science or similar, or equivalent experience
  • 1-2 years building web services (internships count)
  • Solid SQL; Docker and a cloud provider are a plus

Additional Information

Remote within UTC+0 to UTC+5, with three hours of overlap with Hamburg. 28 days of paid leave, home-office budget, learning budget.
