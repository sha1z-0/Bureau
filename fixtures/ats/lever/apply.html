Northwind Labs builds payment orchestration for marketplaces in South Asia and the Gulf: one API that routes card, wallet and bank-transfer payments to the cheapest working rail and reconciles them overnight.

What you'll be doing

  • Building and maintaining Node.js and TypeScript services in our settlement pipeline
  • Writing PostgreSQL migrations and queries that stay correct under concurrent writes
  • Adding observability (metrics, traces, alerts) to the services you own
  • Reviewing code and pairing with the two senior engineers on the team

What we're looking for

  • 1–3 years of professional backend experience
  • Solid TypeScript or JavaScript; some SQL
  • Care for correctness: idempotency, retries and reconciliation should interest you

Nice to have

  • Docker, message queues, any payments or accounting experience
Hybrid: two days a week in our Gulberg, Lahore office.
